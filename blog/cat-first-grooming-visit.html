What to Expect at Your Cat's First Grooming Visit

What to expect at your cat's first professional grooming visit with Petssible in El Paso, from booking ahead to a calmer visit for anxious cats.

Petssible journal

Bringing your cat in for a professional grooming visit for the first time can feel like a bigger step than it is, especially if your cat is more used to home brushing than a grooming table. At Petssible, first cat visits are approached with patience, so your cat has a chance to settle in before any handling begins.

Book ahead and share what your cat needs

Cat grooming works best with a little advance planning. Call (915) 407-5512 or request a visit online, and mention any handling sensitivities, matting, or anxiety your cat has so the team can plan a calmer approach for your specific cat. Cat grooming availability has typically been on Thursdays and Saturdays — call ahead to confirm the current schedule before you book.

What actually happens at the visit

Every cat has different coat, skin, and comfort needs, so the team tailors the visit rather than running through a fixed routine. Depending on what your cat needs, a visit may include a bath, deshedding, nail trims, or a trim/haircut. If your cat has mats, let the team know before you arrive so they can plan the right approach for your cat's coat and comfort.

Petssible offers cat grooming in El Paso, along with nail trims and deshedding for cats who need extra coat care between visits. Call or send a request when you are ready to book your cat's first visit.

Questions about your cat's first visit?

Call Petssible at (915) 407-5512, or request a preferred time online.
