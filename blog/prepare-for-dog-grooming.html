How to Prepare for a Dog Grooming Visit

Simple ways El Paso pet parents can prepare their dog for a calm, comfortable grooming visit.

Petssible journal

A relaxed grooming visit begins before your dog arrives. A little preparation can help the team understand your dog, make check-in easier, and support a calmer experience for everyone.

Share the important details

Tell the groomer about your dog’s coat, sensitivities, previous grooming experiences, and the style you want. If there is a particular area your dog dislikes being touched, mention it early. Clear communication helps the team shape the visit around your pet.

Plan the day around your pet

Give your dog a chance to use the restroom and arrive with a little time to settle. Bring any information the team requested, and make sure your phone number is current. For puppies or nervous pets, an early positive experience matters more than rushing a complicated haircut.
