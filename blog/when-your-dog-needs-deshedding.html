When Does a Dog Need Deshedding?

Signs that your dog may benefit from professional deshedding and a coat-care visit in El Paso.

Petssible journal

Shedding can be part of everyday life with a dog, but excess loose coat can make your home, furniture, and dog less comfortable. A deshedding visit focuses on lifting loose undercoat and refreshing the coat with the right bath, drying, and brushing process.

Common signs it is time

Fur collecting around the house, a dull-looking coat, or more loose hair after brushing can all be signs that your dog needs extra coat care. Seasonal changes can make the issue more noticeable. A professional can help decide whether deshedding, a bath and brush, or a full grooming visit makes the most sense.

Keep home care simple

Between visits, use a brush suited to your dog’s coat and keep the routine gentle. Avoid pulling at mats or using tools that may irritate the skin. If you are unsure what your dog needs, call Petssible before booking.
