Booking & Visiting Petssible

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Petssible asks that you request a visit online or call (915) 407-5512 ahead of time for grooming, daycare, and overnight stays, so the team can plan the right time for your pet.

How do I book an appointment?

Request a visit through the online booking form, or call (915) 407-5512 during business hours and the team will help you find a time.

What are Petssible's hours?

Petssible is open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM, and Saturday, 9 AM to 4 PM.

Where is Petssible located?

Petssible is at 369 Shadow Mountain Dr Ste B in West El Paso, TX 79912, convenient for pet owners across the El Paso area.

Do you groom both dogs and cats?

Yes. Petssible offers grooming for both dogs and cats — see Dog Grooming and Cat Grooming for details on each, along with daycare and overnight stays for dogs.

Dog Grooming FAQs

What is included in a dog grooming appointment?

A Petssible dog grooming visit is built around a bath, blow-dry, coat care, and trimming. Nail trims are offered separately — ask about adding one on when you book.

How long does a dog grooming appointment take?

Time varies by coat type, size, and condition. Call (915) 407-5512 and the team can give you a better estimate for your dog.

How often should I get my dog groomed?

It depends on coat type and lifestyle — see Petssible's grooming frequency guide for a fuller answer, or ask the team for a recommendation based on your dog.

My dog has mats or heavy shedding — can you still groom them?

Let the team know about matting or shedding before your visit. Petssible also offers a dedicated deshedding service for coats that shed heavily or seasonally.

Is Petssible good with nervous or first-time dogs?

Gentle handling and letting each dog settle in are part of how Petssible approaches every visit. Mention any anxiety or sensitivities when you book so the team can plan accordingly.

Cat Grooming FAQs

What is included in a cat grooming visit?

Every cat has different coat, skin, and comfort needs. Call Petssible so the team can recommend the right service — bathing, deshedding, nail trims, or a trim/haircut.

What days is cat grooming available?

Cat grooming availability has typically been on Thursdays and Saturdays — call (915) 407-5512 to confirm the current schedule.

Is Petssible good with anxious cats?

Mention any anxiety or handling sensitivities when you book so the team can plan a calmer visit for your cat.

Bath & Brush, Deshedding & Nail Trims

How is bath & brush different from a full groom?

Bath & brush covers washing, drying, and brushing without a haircut or trim — see dog grooming for the full service.

How is deshedding different from a regular bath?

Deshedding is more focused on thoroughly removing loose undercoat, compared to a standard bath & brush. See when your dog needs deshedding for common signs.

How often do pets need nail trims?

It varies by pet and activity level. Call Petssible so the team can suggest a schedule for your pet.

Do you offer nail polish?

Pet-safe nail polish has been part of past nail trim visits — ask about it when you book.

Dog Daycare & Overnight Stays

What time should I drop off my dog for daycare?

To help establish group play, Petssible asks for dog drop-off by 9 AM. Call ahead to ask about an exit bath or a different schedule. See the daycare checklist for more tips.

What should I bring for daycare?

Something that smells like home, such as a blanket, T-shirt, or even a sock, can help your dog feel more comfortable while away from you.

Can I combine an overnight stay with grooming?

Ask the team when you book about combining an overnight stay with a grooming visit.
