A dog groomer for El Paso pet owners

Searching for a dog groomer or pet groomer in El Paso usually starts with the same question: is there a place nearby that will take genuine care of your pet? Petssible is based at 369 Shadow Mountain Dr Ste B in West El Paso, and pet owners drive in from all over the city — from Northwest El Paso and the Upper Valley to Canutillo and Sunland Park — for grooming, spa care, and daycare.

Grooming and pet care services

Petssible's core services are dog grooming, cat grooming, and puppy grooming, along with more focused visits like bath & brush, deshedding, and nail trims. For pet owners who need more than a grooming appointment, Petssible also offers daycare and overnight stays.

One location, easy to reach from across El Paso

Rather than operating multiple locations, Petssible works from a single home base on Shadow Mountain Dr, which keeps the team, equipment, and standard of care consistent for every pet. The location sits in West El Paso, within a reasonable drive of most of the city — see the service areas overview for area-specific pages with directions context.
