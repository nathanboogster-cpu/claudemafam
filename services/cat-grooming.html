Cat grooming with a caring approach

Cats have their own grooming needs — most cats self-groom day to day, but many still benefit from professional help with bathing, deshedding, nail trims, or a haircut, especially long-haired breeds or cats that struggle to keep mats and tangles under control on their own. Petssible offers cat appointments with the same patient, unhurried approach used for dogs. Reviews mention cat-grooming availability on Thursdays and Saturdays, so please call to confirm the current schedule.

What a cat grooming visit can include

Depending on what your cat needs, a visit can include a bath, brushing and deshedding to work through loose fur and mats, nail trims, and a trim or haircut for cats with longer or harder-to-manage coats. Cat appointments at Petssible are handled by Cynthia, the shop's dedicated cat specialist.

Handling cats calmly

Cats can be more sensitive to handling and new environments than dogs. Let the team know if your cat gets anxious, has specific handling needs, or has never been professionally groomed before — that helps them plan a calmer visit.

Long-haired and mat-prone cats

Breeds with longer coats are more prone to matting, especially around the legs, belly, and behind the ears. If your cat's coat has mats, mention it when you book so the team can plan the right approach. Petssible's deshedding service is also worth asking about for cats that shed heavily.

Plan your pet's appointment

Call (915) 407-5512 to ask about timing, preparation, and availability. The team can also help you choose between related services such as bath and brush care, deshedding, or nail trims.
