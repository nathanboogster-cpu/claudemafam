Deshedding with a caring approach

Seasonal shedding can be a lot to manage, especially for dogs and cats with thick undercoats. A focused deshedding visit helps lift loose coat that regular brushing at home doesn't fully reach, while keeping your pet comfortable throughout.

Signs your pet may need a deshedding visit

Heavier-than-usual shedding, a dull or dusty-looking coat, or loose fur that seems constant rather than seasonal are all reasons to consider a deshedding appointment. El Paso's dry desert climate can also affect coat and skin condition, which is worth mentioning if your pet's shedding seems tied to the season.

How deshedding differs from a regular bath

A deshedding visit is more focused than a standard bath & brush — the emphasis is on thoroughly working through the undercoat to remove loose hair, rather than just washing and drying. It pairs well with a full dog grooming or cat grooming visit for pets that need both.

Plan your pet's appointment

Call (915) 407-5512 to ask about timing, preparation, and availability. The team can also help you choose between related services such as bath and brush care or nail trims.
