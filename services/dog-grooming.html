Dog grooming with a caring approach

A complete grooming visit shaped around your dog, from a careful bath and blow-dry to coat care, trimming, and a clean finish. Petssible focuses on a clean, welcoming environment and takes time to understand your pet's needs before the visit, whether you're bringing in a bright-eyed puppy, a senior dog, or a coat that's overdue for attention.

Petssible is located at 369 Shadow Mountain Dr Ste B in West El Paso, and grooms dogs from across the El Paso area, including Northwest El Paso and the Upper Valley.

What a grooming visit includes

A standard Petssible dog grooming appointment is built around bathing, blow-drying, coat care, and trimming. The team washes and dries each dog thoroughly before working on the coat, then finishes with a trim shaped around the dog's coat type and the look you're going for.

Bathing and drying

Every visit starts with a bath suited to the dog's coat and skin, followed by a full blow-dry. Getting the coat completely dry matters for comfort and for how the coat looks and feels afterward, especially on dogs with thicker or double coats.

Brushing and coat care

Brushing before and after the bath helps work through loose hair and everyday tangles so the coat is easier to manage between visits. For dogs that shed heavily or mat easily, Petssible's deshedding service is available as a more focused option.

Trimming and finishing

Once the coat is clean, dry, and brushed out, the team trims and shapes it for a clean, comfortable finish. If you have a specific look in mind, or your dog just needs a manageable length for El Paso's warmer months, mention it when you request your appointment.

Nail trims and other add-ons

Nail care is offered as its own Petssible service rather than an automatic part of every grooming visit — see Nail Trims for details, or ask about adding it on when you book.

Different coats, different dogs

Matted or heavily shed coats

A coat with mats or heavy shedding usually needs a more careful approach than a routine bath and trim. Let the team know about matting or shedding concerns before your visit so they can plan accordingly, and consider Petssible's deshedding service for dogs that shed year-round or seasonally.

Puppies and first-time grooms

A first grooming visit is really about helping a puppy get comfortable with the experience. Petssible's puppy grooming page has more on what a calm, patient first visit looks like.

Senior dogs

Older dogs can need a gentler pace and more frequent breaks. Mention your dog's age and any mobility or health considerations when you request an appointment so the visit can be planned around them.

Nervous or anxious dogs

Petssible's approach starts with gentle handling and letting each dog settle in before any work begins. If your dog tends to get anxious around grooming, tell the team ahead of time — it helps them plan a calmer visit.

How often should a dog be groomed?

Grooming frequency depends on coat type, activity level, and how much home brushing a dog gets between visits. Petssible's guide on how often to groom your dog walks through the factors that matter most, and the team can recommend a schedule based on your dog specifically.

Preparing for your appointment

A little preparation makes for a smoother visit. See Petssible's guide on preparing for a dog grooming visit for tips on what to bring and how to help your dog feel ready.

Why professional grooming matters

Regular grooming does more than keep a dog looking good — routine brushing, bathing, and coat maintenance help catch mats, skin irritation, and coat problems early, and keep a dog more comfortable day to day. It's also a chance for the Petssible team to get to know your dog and flag anything worth mentioning to your vet.

Dog Grooming FAQs

What is included in a dog grooming appointment?

A Petssible dog grooming visit is built around a bath, blow-dry, coat care, and trimming. Nail trims are offered separately — ask about adding one on when you book.

How long does a grooming appointment take?

Time varies by coat type, size, and condition. Call (915) 407-5512 and the team can give you a better estimate for your dog.

How often should I get my dog groomed?

It depends on coat type and lifestyle — see Petssible's grooming frequency guide for a fuller answer, or ask the team for a recommendation based on your dog.

My dog has mats or heavy shedding — can you still groom them?

Let the team know about matting or shedding before your visit. Petssible also offers a dedicated deshedding service for coats that shed heavily or seasonally.

Is Petssible good with nervous or first-time dogs?

Gentle handling and letting each dog settle in are part of how Petssible approaches every visit. Mention any anxiety or sensitivities when you book so the team can plan accordingly.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — request a visit online or call ahead to discuss availability and book a time.
