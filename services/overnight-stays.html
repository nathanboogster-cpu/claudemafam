Overnight stays with a caring approach

When you need overnight care — a weekend trip, travel, or just a night away — Petssible offers a familiar place for your dog to stay instead of an unfamiliar kennel. Contact the team to discuss dates, routines, and availability.

Helping your dog feel settled overnight

A dog's normal routine, food, and any comfort items can make a real difference for an overnight stay. Share your dog's usual schedule, feeding routine, and any medications or sensitivities when you book, so the team can keep things as close to normal as possible.

Combining overnight stays with grooming or daycare

Ask the team when you book about combining an overnight stay with daycare or a grooming visit.

Plan your pet's stay

Call (915) 407-5512 to ask about timing, preparation, and availability. The team can also help you choose between related services such as daycare or dog grooming before or after the stay.
